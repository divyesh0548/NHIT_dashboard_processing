Module A — Traffic Volume & Flow
Hourly/daily/monthly vehicle count by lane and class (Done)

--> Peak vs. off-peak distribution (identify staffing/lane-opening mismatches)

Benefit: Detects lanes kept closed during high-traffic windows (revenue suppression) or artificially inflated "low traffic" claims used to justify toll hikes/subsidies.


Module B — Vehicle Classification Mix
% share by class (Car/LCV/Bus/Truck/Multi-axle) (Done)

--> Classification accuracy cross-check (WIM data vs. charged class)

Benefit: Under-classification (e.g., multi-axle truck billed as LCV) is the single largest revenue leakage vector in Indian toll audits. This is your #1 audit flag.


Module C — Headway/Gap Analysis   (average of time gap per lane and also average of vehicle)

Average and distribution of time-gap between vehicles per lane

--> Flag gaps below physical minimum (e.g., <2 sec = potential tailgating fraud or sensor miscounting)
--> Flag abnormally long gaps during peak hours (lane malfunction or deliberate slow-processing)
Benefit: This is your primary fraud-detection layer — tailgating (multiple vehicles passing on one transaction) is undetectable from revenue totals alone; only headway data exposes it.


Module D — Transaction Mode Reconciliation  (use VRN files for this)
FASTag vs. Cash vs. Exempt ratio, trended over time  (Done)
--> Cash-lane revenue per shift vs. expected volume (from ANPR independent count)
Benefit: Cash-lane leakage (operator collusion, unrecorded cash) is exposed only when compared against an independent (camera/ANPR) vehicle count — not the cash register itself.


Module E — Exempt/VIP Pass Audit
Volume and % of exempt-category vehicles by lane, by time
Trend over time (spikes = misuse)
Benefit: Exempt pass abuse is a common concealment route for revenue diversion; sudden spikes on specific lanes/shifts indicate manual override abuse.


Module F — Revenue Reconciliation
Expected revenue (traffic count × class-wise toll rate) vs. Actual reported revenue
Variance %, by lane, by day, by shift
Benefit: This is the audit's bottom line — the variance is your quantified leakage estimate. Every other module feeds into explaining why the variance exists.


Module G — Anomaly & Outlier Flags
Statistical outliers: sudden volume drops, classification shifts, unusual exempt spikes
Lane downtime logs (system offline periods) cross-checked against manual toll collection records during downtime
Benefit: System downtime is a known window for manual, unrecorded cash collection — must be independently verified.
